About the Programme

Wounded Military Heroes Special Recognition

The Wounded Military Heroes Special Recognition Programme is designed to recognize military personnel who have sustained serious injuries or medical conditions while serving their country.

We understand that some wounded soldiers may be physically unable to access the website or complete the award application process themselves. Therefore, an authorized husband, wife, family member, or legal representative may request permission to manage the application on behalf of the injured soldier.

Our purpose is to ensure that wounded military heroes receive dignity, respect, recognition, and appropriate support for their service and sacrifice.

Application Process

1. Representative Request

The husband or authorized family member requests representative access.

2. Identity Verification

The soldier's identity and military batch number are verified.

3. Documentation Review

Supporting medical and service documentation is reviewed securely.

4. Eligibility Determination

The responsible authority determines eligibility.

5. Decision Communication

The award decision is communicated to the representative.

6. Formal Issuance

Approved awards and recognition documents are formally issued.

7. Ongoing Wellness Check

Post-award follow-ups to ensure the wounded hero's continued recovery, adjusting support as needed.

A Voice From the Wounded

My Message as a Wounded Female Soldier

FROM THE DESK OF THE SOLDIER

I am a female military personnel who sustained injuries while serving my country. Due to my present medical condition, I may not always be physically capable of handling administrative processes or accessing the award portal myself.

I therefore wish to authorize my husband to represent me in the process of submitting my application, providing the necessary documentation, communicating with the relevant authorities, and following up on my recognition request.

My request is for consideration under the Wounded Military Heroes Special Recognition Programme, including the proposed $120,000 financial recognition, Tesla vehicle, Gold Medal of Military Honour, and Certificate of Honour.

I respectfully request that my service record, medical circumstances, and contributions be reviewed by the appropriate authorized institution. I remain proud of my service and grateful for the opportunity to have my sacrifice recognized.

With Honour, Courage, and Dignity.
— The Wounded Female Soldier

Knowledge Center

Resources & FAQ

Find answers to common questions and access helpful resources about the programme.

Who can apply as a representative?
An authorized husband, wife, family member, or legal representative may request permission to manage the application on behalf of the wounded soldier. Actual access requires identity checks, the soldier's consent or lawful authority, and approval by the responsible institution.
Are the awards guaranteed?
All benefits are proposed awards subject to official programme approval, eligibility verification, and confirmation by the responsible awarding institution.
What documents are required?
Required documents may include the soldier's military batch number, service records, medical documentation of injury, and authorization for the representative. All documents are reviewed securely.
How can third parties verify an award?
Authorized independent organizations may use the Verification Portal to confirm the authenticity of an application reference, issued certificate, medal approval, or benefit authorization.
What is included in the recognition package?
The proposed package includes a $120,000 financial recognition award, a Tesla vehicle recognition gift, a Gold Medal of Military Honour, and an official Certificate of Honour — all subject to official approval.
